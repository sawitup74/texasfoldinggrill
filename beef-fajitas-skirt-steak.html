Beef Fajitas With Skirt Steak Over Mesquite

Tex-Mex beef fajitas on a small flat pack charcoal grill: marinated skirt steak seared hot over mesquite, with peppers and onions on the griddle.

Fajitas started as a ranch-hand cut on the Texas border, and they're made for a small grill: skirt steak is thin and cooks in minutes over hot coals. Run a griddle plate on one half for the peppers and onions.

Serves 4Time 30 minutes plus 1–8 hours marinatingGrill Slot Grill Compact

Ingredients

  • 1½–2 lb outside or inside skirt steak
  • Marinade: juice of 2 limes, ¼ cup oil, 3 cloves garlic, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp chili powder, 1 tsp salt, ½ tsp pepper
  • 2 bell peppers and 1 large onion, sliced
  • Flour or corn tortillas
  • Pico de gallo, guacamole, sour cream, lime wedges

Method

  1. Marinate the steak in the fridge 1 to 8 hours. Lime gets mushy past that.
  2. Light a full chimney of lump, with a mesquite chunk. Pour a full, hot bed under the grate half and a thinner layer under the griddle.
  3. Cook peppers and onions on the oiled griddle until charred and tender.
  4. Pat the steak dry. Sear over the hottest coals 2 to 4 minutes per side to at least 145°F (many prefer 130–135°F for medium-rare; 145°F is the USDA safe minimum).
  5. Rest 5 minutes. Slice thin across the grain.
  6. Warm tortillas on the grate 10 seconds a side. Build at the table.

Tips

  • Skirt steak's grain runs across its width; cut long pieces into 5–6" sections first, then slice across.
  • Discard used marinade.

Safe internal temperatures

Check the thickest part with an instant-read thermometer, away from bone. These are USDA minimum safe temperatures; tougher cuts like brisket and ribs are cooked well past them for tenderness.

FoodMinimum internal temperature
Beef, pork, lamb and veal steaks, chops and roasts145°F, then rest 3 minutes
Ground beef, pork, lamb and veal (burgers, fresh sausage)160°F
Chicken and turkey, whole, pieces or ground165°F
Fish and shellfish145°F, or flesh opaque and flakes with a fork
Egg dishes160°F; cook eggs until yolks and whites are firm
Precooked sausage, hot dogs and leftoversReheat to 165°F

Cooler rules for hot Texas days

  • Keep it at 40°F or below; refill ice before it's gone.
  • Raw meat sealed, on the bottom; drinks in a separate cooler so the food lid stays shut.
  • Perishables out no more than 1 hour above 90°F.
  • Separate tongs and platters for raw and cooked.

Look after the steel

Carbon steel and Gulf humidity don't mix unless you keep it oiled. Scrape warm, empty cold ash, wipe the cooking surfaces with a thin coat of oil or Seasoning Balm, and store it dry. More in care in Texas humidity.

Questions people ask

How long should fajita meat marinate?

1 to 8 hours in the fridge; longer in lime juice can make it mushy.
