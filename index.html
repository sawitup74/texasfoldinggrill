Post oak · Salt & pepper · Folding steel

Texas barbecue, cut down to fit the tailgate

You don't need a trailer-mounted offset to cook like a Texan. A small folding charcoal grill, a sack of lump, a few splits of post oak and some patience get you fajitas at the stadium, links at deer camp and real beef on a gravel bar. These guides show how, honestly: what a small lidless grill does well, and how to adapt brisket, sausage and ribs when it can't do a 14-hour cook.
