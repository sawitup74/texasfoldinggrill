Texas Football Tailgate Grilling With a Flat Pack Grill

Setting up a flat pack charcoal grill at a Texas football tailgate: lot rules, menu, timing, and safe ash handling.

Friday night lights, Saturday college games, Sunday pros: in Texas, the parking lot cook is part of the game. A flat pack grill that packs flat behind the seat is made for it.

Before you go

  • Check the venue's tailgating rules. Many lots allow charcoal with conditions (ash containers, distance from vehicles); some don't allow open flames at all.
  • Prep at home: slice fajita meat, skewer, season, make sides.
  • Pack a lidded metal ash can, water, gloves, tongs and a thermometer.

Set up

  • On the pavement, away from the vehicle, fuel areas and foot traffic.
  • Never on a tailgate, plastic table or truck bed.
  • The Slot Grill's air gap (2⅞" on the Compact, 3⅝" on the Full Size) helps minimize radiant heat to the surface below, but it stays on non-combustible ground.

Safe cooking, Texas edition

  • Check the burn ban for the county every trip; see burn bans.
  • Bare dirt, gravel, sand or concrete. Never dry grass, a truck bed, a tailgate or a boat deck.
  • Outdoors only. Charcoal makes carbon monoxide; never use it in a tent, camper, garage, vehicle or boat cabin.
  • Water and a shovel within reach; gloves for hot steel.
  • Drown and stir ash until cold before you leave.

Cooler rules for hot Texas days

  • Keep it at 40°F or below; refill ice before it's gone.
  • Raw meat sealed, on the bottom; drinks in a separate cooler so the food lid stays shut.
  • Perishables out no more than 1 hour above 90°F.
  • Separate tongs and platters for raw and cooked.

Look after the steel

Carbon steel and Gulf humidity don't mix unless you keep it oiled. Scrape warm, empty cold ash, wipe the cooking surfaces with a thin coat of oil or Seasoning Balm, and store it dry. More in care in Texas humidity.

Questions people ask

Can I leave a charcoal grill burning while I'm in the game?

No. Never leave a fire unattended. Let it burn out and handle ash per venue rules.

Do stadiums allow charcoal grills?

Many lots do with conditions, some don't. Check the venue's tailgate policy.
