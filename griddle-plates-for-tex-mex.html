Griddle Plates for Tex-Mex Cooking

How a carbon steel griddle plate on a flat pack grill handles fajitas, breakfast tacos, smash burgers and tortillas.

Tex-Mex lives on the flat top. The Slot Grill Griddle Add-On is a carbon steel plate made for the grill's two-piece top; you can run one plate with one grate, or two plates. It's available for both the Compact and the Full Size, and stacks flat with the grill.

What to cook on it

  • beef fajitas and chicken fajitas
  • Breakfast tacos
  • Chopped beef
  • Warming tortillas, toasting buns, charring onions and peppers

Griddle basics

  1. Season it like the grill.
  2. Preheat over coals; water drops should skitter.
  3. Thin film of oil, then food.
  4. Scrape warm, wipe, oil before stacking.

Look after the steel

Carbon steel and Gulf humidity don't mix unless you keep it oiled. Scrape warm, empty cold ash, wipe the cooking surfaces with a thin coat of oil or Seasoning Balm, and store it dry. More in care in Texas humidity.

Safe cooking, Texas edition

  • Check the burn ban for the county every trip; see burn bans.
  • Bare dirt, gravel, sand or concrete. Never dry grass, a truck bed, a tailgate or a boat deck.
  • Outdoors only. Charcoal makes carbon monoxide; never use it in a tent, camper, garage, vehicle or boat cabin.
  • Water and a shovel within reach; gloves for hot steel.
  • Drown and stir ash until cold before you leave.

Questions people ask

Can I use a griddle and a grate at the same time?

Yes. The two-piece top takes one griddle plate and one grate, or two plates.
