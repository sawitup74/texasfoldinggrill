Hauling a Folding Grill in a Truck, UTV or Boat

How to carry a flat-pack charcoal grill in a pickup, side-by-side or boat, and why you never cook on the vehicle.

A flat pack rides anywhere: behind a truck seat, in a UTV box, under a boat seat. Slot Grill's FAQ confirms both work in a UTV bed or cargo box and aboard a boat as cargo.

Carrying

  • Cold and empty only. Never pack a warm grill or ash.
  • Bag it to keep soot off seats and gear.
  • Strap it flat so it doesn't slide.

Cooking

  • Set up on the ground, clear of the vehicle, dry grass and anything that can burn.
  • Never on a truck bed, tailgate, UTV bed or boat deck.
  • Keep fuel cans far from the fire.

Look after the steel

Carbon steel and Gulf humidity don't mix unless you keep it oiled. Scrape warm, empty cold ash, wipe the cooking surfaces with a thin coat of oil or Seasoning Balm, and store it dry. More in care in Texas humidity.

Safe cooking, Texas edition

  • Check the burn ban for the county every trip; see burn bans.
  • Bare dirt, gravel, sand or concrete. Never dry grass, a truck bed, a tailgate or a boat deck.
  • Outdoors only. Charcoal makes carbon monoxide; never use it in a tent, camper, garage, vehicle or boat cabin.
  • Water and a shovel within reach; gloves for hot steel.
  • Drown and stir ash until cold before you leave.
