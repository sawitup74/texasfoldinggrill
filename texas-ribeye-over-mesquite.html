Texas Ribeye Over Mesquite Coals

A thick ribeye seasoned with salt and pepper, seared over mesquite coals on a flat pack grill and finished on the cool side.

Mesquite coals burn hot and give beef a bold, desert-campfire flavor. A thick ribeye on a two-zone fire is the best showcase.

Serves 2Time 30 minutesGrill Slot Grill Full Size

Ingredients

  • 2 ribeyes, 1–1½" thick
  • Texas salt and pepper rub
  • Butter (optional)

Method

  1. Season 45 minutes ahead or right before cooking.
  2. Light mesquite lump; build a hot two-zone fire.
  3. Sear over the hottest coals 2 to 3 minutes per side.
  4. Move to the cool side; turn every minute or two until 5°F below your target. USDA safe minimum is 145°F plus a 3-minute rest.
  5. Rest 5 minutes. Optional pat of butter on top.

Tips

  • Render the fat edge against the grate with tongs.
  • Sear and slide keeps the outside from burning.

Safe internal temperatures

Check the thickest part with an instant-read thermometer, away from bone. These are USDA minimum safe temperatures; tougher cuts like brisket and ribs are cooked well past them for tenderness.

FoodMinimum internal temperature
Beef, pork, lamb and veal steaks, chops and roasts145°F, then rest 3 minutes
Ground beef, pork, lamb and veal (burgers, fresh sausage)160°F
Chicken and turkey, whole, pieces or ground165°F
Fish and shellfish145°F, or flesh opaque and flakes with a fork
Egg dishes160°F; cook eggs until yolks and whites are firm
Precooked sausage, hot dogs and leftoversReheat to 165°F

Cooler rules for hot Texas days

  • Keep it at 40°F or below; refill ice before it's gone.
  • Raw meat sealed, on the bottom; drinks in a separate cooler so the food lid stays shut.
  • Perishables out no more than 1 hour above 90°F.
  • Separate tongs and platters for raw and cooked.

Look after the steel

Carbon steel and Gulf humidity don't mix unless you keep it oiled. Scrape warm, empty cold ash, wipe the cooking surfaces with a thin coat of oil or Seasoning Balm, and store it dry. More in care in Texas humidity.
