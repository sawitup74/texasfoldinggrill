Texas BBQ Glossary

Plain definitions of Texas barbecue and flat pack grill terms: bark, packer, flat, point, hot links, post oak, two-zone and more.

Bark
The dark, flavorful crust on smoked meat.
Brisket flat
The leaner, thinner muscle of a brisket; the practical size for a small grill.
Brisket point
The fattier, thicker muscle; source of burnt ends.
Packer
A whole brisket, flat and point together.
Dalmatian rub
Salt and coarse black pepper.
SPG
Salt, pepper, garlic.
Hot links
Spicy Texas sausage links.
Post oak
A Texas oak that's the classic Central Texas barbecue wood.
Mesquite
A hot-burning South Texas wood with strong flavor.
Probe-tender
When a thermometer probe slides in with almost no resistance.
Two-zone fire
Coals on one side, none on the other.
Banked fire
All coals at one end, for indirect cooking.
Wrap / Texas crutch
Wrapping meat in foil or paper partway through a cook.
Rest
Holding cooked meat before slicing so juices settle.
Fajita
Originally skirt steak; now grilled meat strips with tortillas.
Griddle (plancha)
A flat steel plate cooked on like a flat top.
Air gap
On the Slot Grill, the space between the table and the bottom of the coal bed.
Tab and slot
How the Slot Grill's steel panels lock together by hand.
