Texas Salt and Pepper Rub (Dalmatian Rub)

The simple Central Texas beef rub: coarse black pepper and kosher salt, ratios, and when to add garlic.

Central Texas brisket is famous for being seasoned with almost nothing: coarse black pepper and salt. Some pitmasters call it a Dalmatian rub for the black-and-white look. It lets beef and smoke do the talking, and it's perfect for small-grill cooks because there's nothing to burn.

The ratios

StyleMix
Classic1 part kosher salt : 1 part coarse (16-mesh) black pepper, by volume
Peppery1 part salt : 2 parts pepper
SPGEqual parts salt, pepper and granulated garlic

How to use it

  • Pat meat dry. Optional: a thin smear of mustard or oil as a binder.
  • Shake on from about a foot above so it lands evenly.
  • For thick beef, season an hour ahead or the night before (uncovered in the fridge).
  • For steaks, fajitas and burgers, season right before cooking or 45+ minutes ahead.

Use it on brisket flat, beef short ribs and a mesquite ribeye.

Fire plan for a small Texas cook

CookFireFuel
Fajitas, steaks, smash burgersFull or two-zone, hotLump charcoal, mesquite chunk
Links, chickenTwo-zone, mediumCharcoal plus oak or pecan
Ribs, brisket flat (smoke stage)Banked at one endCharcoal plus post oak, fed every 30–45 min
Dutch oven braiseSpread, moderateSteady charcoal, fed as needed

Cooler rules for hot Texas days

  • Keep it at 40°F or below; refill ice before it's gone.
  • Raw meat sealed, on the bottom; drinks in a separate cooler so the food lid stays shut.
  • Perishables out no more than 1 hour above 90°F.
  • Separate tongs and platters for raw and cooked.

Look after the steel

Carbon steel and Gulf humidity don't mix unless you keep it oiled. Scrape warm, empty cold ash, wipe the cooking surfaces with a thin coat of oil or Seasoning Balm, and store it dry. More in care in Texas humidity.

Questions people ask

What pepper grind is used for Texas brisket?

Coarse ground black pepper; many cooks use a 16-mesh grind.
