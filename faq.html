Texas Flat Pack Grill FAQ

Answers about Texas-style cooking on a small flat pack charcoal grill: brisket, sausage, wood, burn bans, and the Slot Grill.

Every question from across the site, grouped by topic.

Fire & Wood

Is mesquite too strong for barbecue?

Mesquite is excellent for fast, hot grilling. On long cooks it can get harsh, so many cooks use it sparingly or mix it with oak. Read: Post Oak, Mesquite and Hickory on a Small Grill

What's the best charcoal for Texas-style grilling?

Lump charcoal lights fast and burns hot, good for fajitas and steaks; briquettes burn steadier for longer cooks. Read: Building a Texas Coal Bed in a Flat Pack Grill

Can you smoke meat on a grill with no lid?

You can add real smoke flavor on the cool side of a lidless grill, then finish tough cuts wrapped or in a Dutch oven. It isn't the same as a closed smoker. Read: Managing Heat Without a Lid

How long does wood take to burn down to coals?

Roughly 30 to 45 minutes for small, dry splits. Read: Burning Splits Down to Coals

Can I use a charcoal grill during a Texas burn ban?

It depends on the specific county order and the location. Read the current order and local park rules before lighting. Read: Burn Bans and Fire Rules for Grilling in Texas

How long can food sit out in Texas heat?

Not more than 1 hour when it's above 90°F, per USDA guidance. Read: Grilling in Texas Wind and Summer Heat

Texas BBQ on a Small Grill

What pepper grind is used for Texas brisket?

Coarse ground black pepper; many cooks use a 16-mesh grind. Read: Texas Salt and Pepper Rub (Dalmatian Rub)

Can I cook a whole packer brisket on a small grill?

It's not a good fit for a small lidless grill. A 3–5 lb flat with a smoke-then-braise finish is the practical approach. Read: Brisket Flat on a Small Flat Pack Grill: Smoke, Then Braise

What temperature should fresh sausage reach?

Fresh pork or beef sausage 160°F; poultry sausage 165°F. Reheat precooked sausage to 165°F. Read: Texas Hot Links and Smoked Sausage on a Flat Pack Grill

When are ribs done?

When a probe slides between the bones easily, usually around 195–203°F. They're safe at 145°F but not yet tender. Read: Pork Spare Ribs on a Flat Pack Grill

What short ribs work on a small grill?

Bone-in chuck short ribs in 2–3 inch pieces. Read: Beef Short Ribs on a Flat Pack Grill

How long do brisket leftovers keep?

Refrigerate within 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F); use within 3 to 4 days, and reheat to 165°F. Read: Chopped Beef Sandwiches

Texas Recipes

How long should fajita meat marinate?

1 to 8 hours in the fridge; longer in lime juice can make it mushy. Read: Beef Fajitas With Skirt Steak Over Mesquite

What temperature should venison steaks be cooked to?

At least 145°F with a 3-minute rest for whole cuts; 160°F for ground venison. Read: Venison Backstrap on a Flat Pack Grill

Tailgates, Camp & River

Can I leave a charcoal grill burning while I'm in the game?

No. Never leave a fire unattended. Let it burn out and handle ash per venue rules. Read: Texas Football Tailgate Grilling With a Flat Pack Grill

Do stadiums allow charcoal grills?

Many lots do with conditions, some don't. Check the venue's tailgate policy. Read: Texas Football Tailgate Grilling With a Flat Pack Grill

Can I grill on my boat?

Not with charcoal. Carry the grill as cargo and cook on shore. Read: Lake Weekend Grilling in Texas

Are charcoal grills allowed on Texas beaches?

It depends on the city, county or park. Check local beach rules before you go. Read: Gulf Coast Beach Grilling
