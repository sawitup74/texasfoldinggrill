About Texas Flat Pack Grill

What Texas Flat Pack Grill covers, who publishes it, and where product facts come from.

Texas Flat Pack Grill is about cooking Texas-style on a small, flat pack charcoal grill: the wood, the fire, the beef and sausage, and the trips where Texans cook.

Who publishes it

This site is published by Slot Grill. The Slot Grill is a collapsible carbon steel charcoal grill made in the USA by Keith in Buna, Texas, in a Compact and a Full Size with an optional griddle. Every cook here is written for it.

How we handle facts

  • Grill specs and prices come from SlotGrill.com, which is always the authority.
  • Safe temperatures follow USDA minimums.
  • No invented reviews, ratings or testimonials.
