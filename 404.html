★TEXAS Folding GRILL

Texas Folding Grill covers Texas-style cooking on a small, folding charcoal grill: post oak and mesquite, beef and sausage, tailgates, deer leases and river weekends. It's published by Slot Grill, which builds its grills in Buna, Texas.

Slot Grill is a collapsible carbon steel charcoal grill made in the USA by Keith in Buna, Texas. Slot Grill® is a registered trademark.
